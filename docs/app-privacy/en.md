# NexGrid App Privacy Policy

## 1. Scope and operator

NexGrid LTD is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. This policy covers the NexGrid App and its H5 web application, including account services, device services, wallet functions and support. The marketing website has a separate [Website Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy/).

## 2. Account and profile information

We process your telephone country code, phone number, password, verification codes and authentication records to register and authenticate your account, recover access and protect it. If you choose a supported external sign-in method, we process the sign-in result and account information supplied through that method to establish your session.

We process your account identifier, nickname, chosen avatar, display language and referral code or relationship to maintain your profile, attribute invitations and provide account features. An avatar is uploaded only when you choose to submit it. Optional profile information is not required to browse the marketing website.

## 3. Transactions, devices and participation

When you use account features, we process the associated orders, device allocations and activation status, balances, transaction amounts and times, transaction identifiers, payout addresses and networks, payment-method references, and request status. These records support account administration, settlement, reconciliation and help with disputed or failed transactions. A payment-method reference is different from a complete payment-card number.

We also process participation records for tasks, rewards, learning activities, invitations, team relationships and rankings. Developer-access applications include company, email and proposed use case; community-event applications include city, event date, budget and category. These details are used to provide the selected feature and review the application.

Device assessment can send model, brand, operating system, processor and memory information, graphics capability, pixel density, network reachability and latency, battery level and charging state where available. We use these observations to assess device capability and provide device services; some measurements may be unavailable on a particular platform.

## 4. Connection, security and usage information

Connections expose technical information such as IP address and browser or device information to the service. Account security features process session identifiers, device names and activity times. Device security reports can include a device identifier, model, operating system, browser or user agent, first-seen and installation times, session counts, foreground duration and automation or display-anomaly signals. These support access control, abuse detection and service availability decisions.

App usage analytics send page routes, visit duration, clicked element or page area, relative click coordinates, event and session identifiers, device category, language and timestamps. They help us understand navigation and improve the service. The analytics event fields do not include form text; page-route values omit query strings and fragments. This does not mean that account activity is anonymous or that the separate App follows the marketing website's analytics settings.

## 5. Support and Nova

When you contact support, we process your message, ticket subject and category, conversation identifiers, timestamps and follow-up records to handle the request. Nova processes the text you send, your selected language, conversation and message identifiers, replies and conversation history to provide AI-assisted responses. Human support and Nova have separate conversation threads.

Only include information needed for the request. Do not send passwords, verification codes, wallet private keys or recovery phrases in a support or Nova message. Information about any external AI processor and its handling of conversation data forms part of the processing arrangements described in section 8.

## 6. Device access and local storage

Choosing an avatar can open the camera or image picker. Saving a generated poster or proof image can use the device's photo-saving function. Copy actions write the selected address, link or text to the clipboard. System and network information is used for device assessment and interface operation. The access available depends on the operating system and the feature selected.

Where the operating system requests permission, you can decline or later change it in system settings; the corresponding camera, image-selection or saving feature may then be unavailable. Permission settings do not themselves erase information already submitted.

The App uses local storage and session mechanisms for sign-in state, preferences, device identification, pending operations and account-scoped state. Clearing browser or App storage removes local data but does not delete the server account, cancel a transaction or complete an account-deletion request. Signing out is also different from account deletion.

## 7. Sharing and visibility

Relevant team features display member identifiers, names or nicknames, avatars, rank and relationship information to other members with access to those features. Invitation and sharing actions can disclose the information included in the generated link or image to its recipients. Review that content before sharing it.

Transactions submitted to a public blockchain disclose transaction information, including addresses and transaction identifiers, to that network. Public blockchain records are outside NexGrid's ability to erase or alter.

Personnel handling account operations, security or support process information needed for those tasks. Service providers involved in hosting, authentication, message delivery, payments or AI processing may process information required for the service they supply. Following an external link or using an external sign-in or payment service also subjects that interaction to the relevant provider's privacy terms.

## 8. US processing

Our service providers are based in the United States, and information handled for the App is stored and processed there. Accessing the App from another country involves transferring information to the United States. The external links and public blockchain records described in section 7 are governed by their respective services and networks.

We use information to deliver requested services, maintain security, meet applicable obligations and, where required, act on consent. Our policy requires access to be limited to personnel and providers who need it for the stated purpose, with appropriate access controls, protected transmission and secure disposal. Providers processing information on our behalf must follow our instructions and equivalent confidentiality and security requirements. We do not authorize them to sell App personal information, use it for their own advertising, or train general-purpose AI models on Nova conversations. We do not sell App personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Requests from public authorities are assessed for a valid legal basis and limited to the information required. Where applicable law requires additional safeguards for an international transfer, our policy requires those safeguards to be in place. Contact us for information about the providers and transfer arrangements relevant to your request.

## 9. Retention and account deletion

The following retention policy applies to information held by us and processors acting on our behalf. For ordinary records, use the earlier of the category's limit and the applicable accepted-deletion or account-deletion cleanup deadline. Backups and specific exception records follow their separate rules below.

- Account and profile information, device and participation records: while needed to provide the account's services; after account deletion is completed, retain for one calendar month, then delete or irreversibly anonymize. Information held during that month is not used for account-facing features.
- Ordinary usage analytics, connection and security logs: up to 90 days from collection. At that point, delete or irreversibly anonymize them. Statistics that no longer identify or permit re-identification of an individual may be kept longer.
- Support tickets and Nova conversation history: up to 12 months after the last interaction, including for tickets that remain open, for follow-up and service continuity. The earlier-deletion rule above applies. Records needed for a continuing investigation must qualify for a documented exception below.
- Backups: after deletion from active systems, residual copies expire within a further 90 days. They are restricted to recovery and security purposes, not ordinary use. If restored, applicable deletions must be reapplied before the data returns to ordinary use.
- Financial, transaction, dispute and security-incident records: retain only the necessary records for an applicable statutory period or a documented unresolved claim, investigation or legal hold. For records subject to a five-year financial-recordkeeping rule, that period runs from the event specified by the applicable rule; it is not a five-year rule for every account field. Review exception records at least annually and delete or irreversibly anonymize them when the reason ends and applicable periods expire. A hold does not extend retention of unrelated information.

If an exception prevents deletion, we explain the affected categories, reason and applicable period or criteria, unless disclosure is legally prohibited. Public blockchain records remain subject to section 7. Local data remains on the device until cleared or removed through the relevant App, browser or operating-system controls.

To request account deletion, open Me → Security → Submit account-deletion request and verify your current password. The request is reviewed by the service; signing out after submission is not proof that deletion has completed. Pending withdrawals can prevent submission. Review the asset consequences shown in the confirmation before proceeding. For privacy requests or when you cannot access the account, use the contact in section 12.

## 10. Requests and choices

You can use the available profile and security settings to change profile information, manage sessions and submit an account-deletion request. You may also email us, including without access to the App, to request account or personal-information deletion, access, correction or a copy of your information. Depending on applicable law, you may request portability, restriction of processing, object to processing, withdraw consent, or appeal a decision. Withdrawing consent does not affect prior lawful processing; an optional feature that needs the information may stop working.

We may need information sufficient to verify the account and understand the request. Our response target is within 30 days of receipt; if a lawful extension is needed, we explain the reason and expected timing within the applicable initial period. We explain restrictions on a request and how to seek review. You may contact the relevant privacy regulator and will not be treated unfairly for exercising applicable privacy rights. Do not email a password or verification code.

## 11. Age and updates

The service is intended for people aged 18 or older. If personal information has been provided by a child, contact us so the matter can be investigated and addressed. We update this policy when the described practices change and identify the revision date on this page.

## 12. Contact

For NexGrid App privacy and data requests, contact NexGrid LTD at [compliance@nexgrid.ai](mailto:compliance@nexgrid.ai). Include the nature of the request and an account contact or identifier sufficient to locate the account; do not include account secrets.
