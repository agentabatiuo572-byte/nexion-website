## 1. Scope

This policy covers the NexGrid marketing website (the "Site"). The NexGrid application (the "App") has its own privacy policy, shown in the App — data practices inside the App are governed by that document, not this one.

## 2. What this Site collects

The Site has no user accounts and no forms. It does not use advertising trackers, and it loads no third-party analytics scripts.

The Site uses its own analytics to understand how the Site is used. It records: the pages viewed, which sections, buttons and questions were interacted with, the display language, whether the device is a phone or a desktop, a broad category for how the visit arrived (for example: search, social, direct), campaign parameters if they are present in the address, basic page-performance measurements, and the country associated with the connection.

Your IP address is not stored. It is used only to derive a short identifier that lets us count repeat visits within a single day; that identifier is regenerated every day, so visits cannot be linked from one day to the next. No name, email address, account, or other directly identifying information is collected by the Site.

If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal, the Site's analytics do not run at all.

Individual records are deleted after 90 days. Only aggregate daily counts are kept after that.

## 3. Cookies and browser storage

Ordinary browsing sets no cookies. If you reach the Site through a regional access link, a single cookie is set for up to 30 days for the sole purpose of allowing that access; it carries no information about you.

The Site stores one preference in your browser: whether you have turned off the animated background. It stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Your language choice is reflected in the address (for example /vi/ or /zh/) rather than stored on your device.

## 4. Server logs

Our hosting infrastructure may record standard technical logs (such as IP address, user agent, and requested URL) for security and operational purposes. These logs are retained only as long as operationally necessary and are not used to identify individuals.

## 5. External links

The Site links to app stores and to the NexGrid App. Once you leave the Site, the destination's own privacy terms apply.

## 6. Availability by region

The Site is not available in some regions. Availability is determined by the country associated with your connection.

Requests that are turned away are counted, recording only the country and the kind of page requested. No identifier is recorded for them.

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the current version.
