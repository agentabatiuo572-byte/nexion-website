NexGrid is designed so that your first session takes about 90 seconds from install to dashboard.

## 1. Install and open the app

Download NexGrid from the App Store or Google Play (or open the web app). The first screen asks for an email or phone number — no documents, no card.

## 2. Verify and set a password

Enter the verification code, set your password, and complete the quick security check. If you were invited by a friend, their code may already be filled in.

## 3. Claim your welcome gift

New accounts receive a welcome bonus in NEX. Depending on account review, it lands immediately or shows as pending — both states are visible on your dashboard, nothing extra to do.

## 4. You're in

The home screen shows your devices, today's activity, and balances. Next step: turn on phone compute — see the "Turning on phone compute" guide.

> Screenshots follow the current app release (v3.7). If your screen differs slightly after an update, the flow stays the same.
