Alongside USDT, your devices earn UVT — the platform token minted when inference work completes. Three common things to do with it:

## Convert to USDT

Wallet → Exchange converts UVT to USDT at the rate shown on screen. Converted USDT joins your withdrawable balance.

## Offset withdrawal fees

Each USDT withdrawal carries a network confirmation fee. At the withdrawal screen you can choose to burn UVT to cover it — useful when you want your USDT to arrive whole.

## Stake for interest

The staking screen lets you lock UVT for a period and accrue UVT-denominated interest. Terms, durations, and current rates are always shown in-app before you confirm — and staking is optional; unstaked UVT stays available to use any time.

## Where to learn more

[The UVT page on this site](/uvt/) explains the token's sources, uses, and supply mechanics; the app's Trust Center tracks the live network data behind it.
