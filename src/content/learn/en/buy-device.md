Dedicated devices (NexGridBox and NexGridRack lines) multiply your compute capacity. They are hosted in professional datacenters, so buying one is closer to activating a service than receiving a parcel.

## 1. Choose a model

Open the Store tab. Each card shows the price and compute multiplier versus the phone baseline. "Classic" models remain fully supported — newer models simply offer higher capacity.

## 2. Check out

Pay from your in-app balance or the supported payment methods shown at checkout. Orders move through four states: placed → paid → provisioning → activated.

## 3. Activation — no shipping involved

Because devices run in datacenters, there is no courier and nothing to install at home. Provisioning usually completes shortly after payment; your new device then appears on the home screen and starts picking up tasks.

## 4. Where earnings show up

The device card tracks its task activity, and settlements flow into the same USDT balance as your phone earnings — one wallet, every device.

> Upgrading later? Any paid device can be traded in toward a higher-capacity model. The app's trade-in screen shows exactly what your current device offsets.
